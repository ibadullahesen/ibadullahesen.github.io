Account and Data Deletion Request

For the FaceToFace mobile application (developed by AxtarGet), users have the right to request the deletion of their account and associated data.

How to Delete Your Account

You can request account deletion using one of the following methods:

  1. In-App Deletion: Open the FaceToFace app, go to your Profile Settings, and tap the **"Delete Account"** button. This will instantly remove your account and data.
  2. Via Email Request: Send an email to our support team at ibadulla.hesen@gmail.com using your registered email address with the subject "Account Deletion Request". We will process your request within 48 hours.

Types of Data Deleted vs. Kept

  • Data That Is Deleted: Your profile information, username, email, and active chat history/radar sessions are permanently deleted from our servers.
  • Data Retention Period: Once a deletion request is processed, all personal data is removed immediately. Temporary chat logs are wiped out automatically upon session termination.
